This is for Selar books like yours

Selar shows pages as pictures (your book is ~1400+ pages). A bookmark cannot read that. Do not paste any code into Google.

You need a computer with Chrome. Phones cannot auto-listen through a whole Selar flipbook.

How to start (computer)

  1. On your computer, open your book in Chrome: selar.com (browser, not the app).
  2. Keep that Selar tab open.
  3. In this ReadGlass tab, tap Start Auto Listen below.
  4. When Chrome asks what to share, pick the Selar tab (not ReadGlass).
  5. ReadGlass will read the page aloud. When it finishes, click > on Selar to turn the page, then tap Next page here (or press Space).
  6. Repeat until you’re done — or tap Stop.

Controls

Ready when you are.

Tip: put Selar on one half of the screen and ReadGlass on the other.

On your phone?

Auto Listen needs a computer. On phone you can only screenshot a few pages, or use your computer and leave it playing.

Capture page

Selar on phone
  1. Open Selar in your mobile browser — not the Selar app.
  2. Screenshot the page.
  3. Tap Add screenshot below and pick it (you can select several).

On a computer, prefer Auto Listen for long books instead of screenshots.
