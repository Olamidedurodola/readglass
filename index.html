Important about Selar

Selar pages are pictures. The reader pane must run on the Selar page — not inside ReadGlass alone.

Android (Chrome)

The green floating panel opens on your Selar book page after you tap the bookmark — not inside ReadGlass.

  1. Open your book on selar.com in Chrome (not the Selar app).
  2. Tap the star / ⋮ → Bookmark this page.
  3. Open Bookmarks → tap ⋮ on that bookmark → Edit.
  4. Tap Copy Android bookmark below, then paste it into the bookmark’s URL field (replace the whole URL). Name it ReadGlass.
  5. Go back to your book page → open the ReadGlass bookmark.
  6. A green floating panel should appear → tap Start.

If Chrome opens Google search instead of the panel, the bookmark URL was not saved as javascript:… — edit it again and paste the full copied text. Firefox for Android also works well with this bookmark.

Computer (Chrome)

  1. Open your book on selar.com in Chrome.
  2. Go to the page you want to start from.
  3. Press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+J) → open the Console tab.
  4. Tap Copy helper line below, paste into the Console, press Enter.
  5. On the green panel, tap Start. It reads left page, then right, then flips.

Paste into the Selar Console only — not the address bar or Google.

Or: Screen share (manual flip)

  1. Open Selar on the page you want.
  2. Tap Start Auto Listen, share the Selar tab.
  3. Check the preview — it should match what you see on Selar.
  4. After each page, click > on Selar, then Next page here.
